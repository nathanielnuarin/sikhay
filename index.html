FAQ

Common questions.

What happens after the 7-day free trial?
After 7 days you’ll be redirected to the payment page. Choose monthly (₱499) or annual (₱4,490) and pay via GCash, Maya, GrabPay, or credit/debit card through PayMongo. Your data is never deleted — even if you stop subscribing.
Can other shops see my orders and data?
No. Every account’s data is completely isolated at the database level using Supabase row-level security. Other users cannot see your orders, finances, or payroll — not even in principle. Your data belongs only to you.
Can I use this on my phone?
Yes. Sikhay System is a web app — it works on any browser, phone, tablet, or desktop. Open it from any device using your login. No app install needed.
How long does setup take?
Most shop owners are running in under 10 minutes. Sign up, confirm your email, enter your business name, and start adding orders. The system was designed to be immediately useful on day one — no complicated onboarding.
What if I want to cancel?
Contact us at nathanielnuarin@sikhaycreatives.com and we’ll stop future billing right away. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
