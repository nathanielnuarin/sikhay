Connect Your Google Calendar
Paste your Google Calendar embed URL below to view your calendar inside this dashboard. This is a one-time setup.
1
Go to calendar.google.com → click the gear icon → Settings
2
Left sidebar → under "My calendars", click your calendar name
3
Scroll to "Integrate calendar" section
4
Copy the URL inside the Embed Code (starts with https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=)
5
Paste it in the field below and click Save
